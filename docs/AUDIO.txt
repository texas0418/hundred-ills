HUNDRED ILLS - AUDIO
Started 2026-08-21. DECISIONS 39: audio is a major budget line and
Simon's unfair advantage. This file is the sound plan: what the game
needs, what Simon is sourcing (open source), what gets recorded later,
and what the engine does with it all.

PRINCIPLES

  Diegetic first. Every sound in the night is a sound of the night -
  no UI sounds, no stings, no musical score in this pass. Refusals
  stay haptic-only. If the world would not make the sound, the game
  does not make it.

  Deep winter. First lunar month, the longest nights. NO insects, no
  cicadas, no crickets, no frogs, no birdsong, no rain. A winter
  night in a shuttered town is nearly silent - the quiet is the bed
  everything else sits on. A summer ambience would be as wrong here
  as a mountain in a painting.

  Nothing modern. No traffic bleed, no electrical hum, no aircraft,
  no distant machinery. Recordings with modern noise floor are
  rejects however good the foreground.

  The drain reaches the ears. As the fires go out the world loses
  colour; it loses sound the same way. The engine cross-fades each
  ambience toward a processed "drained" twin - muffled, distant,
  wrong - built from the same file, same as the paintings' baked
  drained copies. At zero fires the water still moves but sounds a
  room away.

LICENSING - HARD RULES

  The game is PAID (DECISIONS 53). Only these licenses are usable:
    CC0 / public domain    best, no strings
    CC-BY                  fine, attribution goes in the credits
  NEVER CC-BY-NC (non-commercial - we are commercial), never
  ND-anything, never "free for personal use". On freesound.org,
  filter license to "Creative Commons 0" and "Attribution".
  Keep the source URL and author for every file taken - the credits
  list is built from this file.

DELIVERY

  Drop files in ~/Downloads (the usual flow) or a folder anywhere,
  named or not - Claude sorts, trims, loops, levels, converts and
  lands them in assets/audio/, and builds the drained twins. WAV or
  FLAC preferred, MP3/OGG acceptable if that is what exists. 44.1kHz
  or better. Mono is fine for one-shots; stereo preferred for
  ambience. Long is better than short - loops want 45+ seconds of
  raw material; one-shots want several takes in one file or several
  files (repetition is the enemy: a single clapper strike reused
  four times a minute reads as a sample, five takes read as a man).


=============================================================
TIER 1 - THE CLAPPER AND THE OPENING (source these first)
=============================================================

[S1] THE WATCHMAN'S CLAPPER  梆子
     The most important sound in the game: clock, chapter mark, save
     point (DECISIONS 88/89). A dry wooden strike, outdoors, no
     room. Two weights needed:
       - slow strike: deeper, heavier (the watch count)
       - quick strike: lighter, higher (the point count)
     One good dry close recording of each is enough - the distant
     and echoed versions are processed from it. 3-5 takes per weight.
     Search: bangzi, wooden clapper, wood block strike, temple block,
     muyu (too round but workable), claves (too bright, last resort).

[S2] STILL CANAL WATER (ambience loop)
     Near-still water against stone: slow, irregular, small. NOT
     waves, NOT a babbling stream, NOT ocean. The sound of water
     that is barely moving on a windless night. 60s+ raw.
     Plays at: water, east-water, inland-water (loud), mooring,
     farbank, bank screens (faint).
     Search: lake lapping gentle, harbour water quiet, canal water
     lapping, water against dock night.

[S3] WINTER WIND (ambience loop)
     Low, hollow, intermittent - wind finding lanes and eaves, not
     a storm, not whistling. Should be able to sit quietly for long
     stretches. 60s+ raw.
     Plays at: alley, alley-deep, junction, gatelane, gate (with
     nothing else, the gate is wind and silence).
     Search: cold wind ambience, wind through buildings distant,
     winter wind low howl soft.

[S4] THE NIGHT BED (ambience loop)
     The quietest thing in the game and always on: outdoor night
     room-tone, low air, the sense of a sleeping town. No events in
     it (no dogs, no voices, no wildlife). 60s+.
     Search: night ambience quiet, outdoor room tone night, silent
     village night atmosphere.

[S5] THE BRONZE STUDS  摸釘
     Fingertips on cold bronze: a soft damped metal touch, almost a
     thud, absolutely not a clang or ring. 2-3 takes.
     Plays: the gate touch target, with her first line.
     Search: damped metal tap, metal thud soft, muted bronze touch,
     hand on metal door.


=============================================================
TIER 2 - THE WALK (source when convenient)
=============================================================

[S6] FOOTSTEPS ON WET FLAGSTONE
     Single steps, unhurried, a woman's soft shoe or cloth shoe on
     stone, slightly wet. 6-8 distinct takes. Used 2-3 per phase
     transition, randomized.
     Search: footsteps stone single, slow footsteps flagstone wet,
     footstep cobblestone soft.

[S7] THE BRAZIER (warmth loop)
     Small charcoal fire: soft ember crackle, low, contained. NOT a
     campfire roar. 30s+. Plays at the lantern corner while warmth
     relights her; its presence IS the relight feedback.
     Search: charcoal brazier, embers soft crackle, small coal fire.

[S8] LANTERN PAPER (one-shot, sparse)
     A paper lantern shifting in wind: faint paper flex/rustle with
     perhaps a wooden creak of the bracket. 2-3 takes. Colour for
     lantern and house-lamp screens, rare.
     Search: paper lantern, paper rustle slow, wood bracket creak.


=============================================================
TIER 3 - BANKED FOR LATER WATCHES (grab if seen, no hunt)
=============================================================

[S9]  Two small hardwood blocks dropped on stone (the 擲筊 ask verb,
      when it is built): clatter-settle, many takes.
[S10] Big wingbeats, slow, heavy - a large bird passing once (姑獲鳥
      is heard long before she is anything else). No cawing.
[S11] A single water disturbance: one soft slip of something entering
      still water without splash (水鬼). Not a fish jump.
[S12] Wood creak, structural, slow - the covered bridge underfoot.
[S13] Paper scraps in wind on stone (買路錢, the road money).

RECORDED LATER - NOT OPEN SOURCE (noted so nothing waits on it)
=============================================================

  The watchman's VOICE: the watch calls (一更二點...) and the one
  greeting. A cold bored middle-aged man, Chinese. This is direction
  and casting - Simon's lane - and blocks the mooring greeting beat.

  The distant call of her name (阿秀——), 叫魂, once at the first
  watch's edge and again at the end. The most important voice in the
  game; cast with care. Text renders it until then.


=============================================================
ENGINE (Claude's side, built against placeholders if needed)
=============================================================

  Per-node ambience mix: each node declares levels for water / wind
  / bed (the graph already knows which screens touch water). Cross-
  fade on phase, ~1s, so walking toward the canal brings the water
  up before the screen arrives.

  The clapper scheduler reads the same watch/point engine as the
  visual call and strikes the pattern (slow x watch, quick x point);
  the greeting/save moment stays visual until the voice exists.

  Strike variants rotate; nothing plays the same take twice running.

  Drained twins: each ambience gets a processed muffled copy
  (lowpass + level + slight detune), cross-faded by drainAmount()
  exactly as the paintings cross-fade to their drained versions.

  Files land in assets/audio/ with a manifest in src/content/ so
  test-content can assert every referenced sound exists.
