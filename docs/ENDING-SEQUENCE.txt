HUNDRED ILLS - THE ENDING SEQUENCE
Compiled 2026-08-19. Beat by beat. Assumes the corrected ward rule in
LENGTH.txt: alive gets you into houses, dead gets you into the spirit
layer.

Four movements and three endings. Nothing below invents a new system.
Every beat is an existing mechanic firing for the last time.


=============================================================
MOVEMENT ONE - THE THIRD BRIDGE
=============================================================

THE SETUP, planted across the whole game
Every bridge in the town has a 石敢當 at its head - the stone that
refuses passage, DECISIONS 27. Bridges one and two have theirs intact,
so both are crossed ALIVE, at three fires. The player learns across
eight hours that bridges are a living-state affair.

The third bridge's stone has fallen into the water and broken.

THE CROSSING
So the third bridge is the one you cross DEAD. The player must put out
all three fires on purpose. By now this is routine - they have done it
a hundred times, it is just how you get into the other half of a place.
There is no dread attached to it. That is deliberate.

The bridge is covered, roofed, enclosed. The only one you cannot see
through, per PROMPTS.txt. It is dark and it is long.

THE GESTURE
Midway there is a gap in the boards where people look down at the
water. She leans over it. This is the habitual action of the entire
game - lean over water, count your flames, DECISIONS 67. The player has
done it hundreds of times and will do it here without being asked.

She is at zero. There is no reflection. The player expects no
reflection. That is the trap: the absence is not the reveal.

There is a body in the water. Face up. In her clothes.

No sting. No music cue. No text. No camera move. The player understands
here, roughly ten minutes early, exactly as DECISIONS 68 and 69 require.
She does not understand. She straightens up and keeps walking, because
she has somewhere to be.


=============================================================
MOVEMENT TWO - THE LAST STRETCH
=============================================================

She is in the third district, which is mostly dead town, at zero fires,
and she has to get home.

THE FLIP MADE VISIBLE
Every creature she passes now steps aside. The drowned does not reach
for her. The painted skin turns its face away. The night bird does not
descend. For eight hours the player read this as menace barely held
off. Now, in one uninterrupted walk, they watch it be deference.

This is DECISIONS 65 paying out in real time, and it costs no new art -
it is the same creature plates with different behaviour flags.

THE SCRAMBLE
Her own door will have intact wards. To pass them she must be at THREE
fires. So the finale is mechanical and it is desperate: relight all
three in a district that is mostly dead, where living hearths are
almost nonexistent. Every relighting trick the game has taught gets
used one last time, under pressure, in the wrong place for it.

The player will work hard at this. They will feel competent. They are
racing to make her alive enough to get in.

THE VOICE
叫魂. It has been distant all night, once per watch, unremarked. Now it
is a street away and it is unmistakably a name, and it is hers, and it
is a living person calling.

She hurries toward it. Nothing threatens her. Nothing has threatened
her for twenty minutes. That is the most frightening stretch in the
game and it contains no threat at all.


=============================================================
MOVEMENT THREE - THE DOOR
=============================================================

She arrives at three fires. Her own house.

The door gods on it are ELEVEN DAYS OLD. Pasted up at New Year,
uncreased, unfaded, bright. In a game full of torn and peeling wards,
this is the only perfect pair left, and it is on her house, because her
family is the one household with something to protect.

THE WARDS HOLD.

Three fires is not enough. It was never a key - it was a costume, and
the gods are not fooled by a costume. She cannot cross. She stands on
her own doorstep and cannot cross.

The lamp is lit in the window. It has been lit in every scene she has
been visible in since 一更, and nobody has ever remarked on it.

The voice calls her name again, from inside, six feet away.

AND THE GAME WAITS.

No prompt. No button. No timer. No indicator of any kind. The player
sits in front of a still image of a door and a lamp and a name being
called, and the game does not tell them what it wants.


=============================================================
MOVEMENT FOUR - THE THREE ENDINGS
=============================================================

ENDING ONE - SHE ANSWERS
The player taps. Answering to your name costs a fire; the player has
known that cold for eight hours, and here it is not a cost. It is the
door opening.

The wards do not fail. Nothing breaks them. A soul called home by its
own family is not an intruder - that is what 叫魂 IS, and the game has
been showing the player the mechanism all night. Her family has spent
the entire night calling her past her own protection, out of love,
because they want her back.

She goes in. Cut. The bird is inside the house with the child.

ENDING TWO - SHE DOES NOT ANSWER
The player does nothing, which is the trained-correct play of the
entire game, and keeps doing nothing.

Dawn. Cockcrow, per DECISIONS 7 and 15. The lamp goes out because it is
morning. The wards held. She is still outside.

The child lives. She is not released - she is exactly what she was, and
per DECISIONS 78 and the coda at ENDING-SITE.txt option E, she will
walk again next year. Someone will read her in the almanac.

ENDING THREE - THE RELEASE, second walk only
Only available if the player did the 破血湖 work across the game, which
requires knowing the Blood Pool material exists, which requires the
almanac reading of DECISIONS 82 and 77 that nobody completes first
time through.

A third option stands at the door: not in, not waiting - away, to the
temple, to the rite her family never bought her.

She is released. It costs her the child, permanently and knowingly.
It is the only ending in which she rests, and it is the only one she
chooses with full knowledge of what she is.


=============================================================
WHY THE FINAL INPUT WORKS
=============================================================

The last decision in the game is between DOING NOTHING and DOING THE
ONE THING THE GAME SPENT EIGHT HOURS PUNISHING.

Doing nothing is the trained play, the disciplined play, the play a
good player makes automatically - and it is mercy.
Answering is the human play, the one every instinct wants - and it is
catastrophe.

The game never says which it prefers, has no fail state, and does not
grade you. Both are complete. Both are correct readings of the folklore.

And whichever the player chooses, they chose it about a woman they had
understood for twenty minutes and she had not.

WHAT THIS FORCES BACK UPSTREAM
=============================================================

Additions to the 一更 planting list in ENDING-SITE.txt:

  7. The 石敢當 at the head of bridge one, intact and unremarkable, so
     that a broken one reads as broken.
  8. Someone's door gods being pasted up, in a flashback or as a
     neighbour's chore, so the player knows they are eleven days old.
  9. At least one relighting taught in a place where it is easy, so
     that doing it in the dead district is recognisably the same act
     under worse conditions.

And one hard constraint: the lamp in her window must be visible in
every single scene it could plausibly be visible in, from the first
watch, and NO character, line, or camera may ever draw attention to it.
If it is remarked on once, the ending is spoiled.
