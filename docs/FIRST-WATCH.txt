HUNDRED ILLS - THE FIRST WATCH, 一更二點 TO 一更五點
Draft 1, 2026-08-23. Continues docs/OPENING.txt (一更一點, ratified
and shipped through b61). This is the rest of the first watch - the
ninety minutes after the watchman's greeting, in the first district
as it now stands under DECISIONS 110. Written to be torn apart. Every
proposed line is [C] and must survive 65's two readings.

THE TEST this file has to pass (LENGTH.txt): name the authored thing
in every stretch. The first watch's are: the first ward, the first
creature, the ask verb, the first relight, and the voice. One per
point, in that order.

WHAT THE FIRST WATCH TEACHES, without saying it: that the town has
two states and she chooses between them (84). By the 二更 card the
player should have lost a fire, been refused somewhere because of it,
found warmth, been let through, and - if they went all the way down -
been told by something drowned that she is no use to it.


=============================================================
THE VERBS - what the hands do by the end of the watch
=============================================================

Three exist: SWIPE walks, TOUCH acts on a thing in the painting (the
studs, a fork's mouth, the water to lean), and double-tap looks back.
The watch adds the ask verb, and this draft proposes changing how
looking back is done - see OPEN QUESTION 1. The budget is four hand
verbs for the whole game and no more: swipe, touch, hold, throw.

  SWIPE   walk. Unchanged.
  TOUCH   act on a thing. Unchanged.
  HOLD    look back (proposed). Press and hold anywhere: she stops
          and turns. The screen she came from shows for a moment
          behind her, DRAINED - the dead town is what you see when
          you look behind you - then she faces front and a fire is
          out. A hold is the natural accidental input, which is right:
          people break this rule by accident. The double-tap is
          undiscoverable and never felt like turning.
  THROW   ask (once she has the blocks). Tap the empty ground at her
          feet: the blocks clatter and land, 聖筊 / 笑筊 / 陰筊. The
          question is never typed; it is always the same question,
          asked of the place she stands: "this way?" - and the god
          laughs at places where that is the wrong question to ask.


=============================================================
一更二點 - THE FIRST WARD, AND THE FIRST LOSS
=============================================================

THE AUTHORED THING: a door that is open to the living and shut to
the dead, met while she is still whole - so the player sees it open
before they ever see it shut.

WHERE: bank east, the door under the small red lantern (the painting
already has it). Intact door gods, bright, this year's. At three fires
the door is simply a door she could enter; she does not - it is not
hers - but the gods on it are TOUCHABLE and touching them gives her
first ward line:

    門神還新得很。是好人家。
    "The door gods are still new. A good household."

Nothing else. She passes. The player has been shown an intact ward
and a warm house behind it, and told nothing.

THE FIRST LOSS, the same point: the road money. Scattered paper on
the flagstones at the bank's far end [44], where the lane runs out
into mist - the funeral route goes where she cannot yet go (72,
planted, not explained). If the player touches the scraps:

    買路錢。別碰，不是給活人的。
    "Road money. Leave it - it's not for the living."

and she does not pick it up. No cost. But the FIRST LOOK BACK is
seeded here: as she turns from the dead end, the clapper sounds
behind her once, close - the watchman, going the other way. A player
who holds to look (or, today, double-taps) sees the bank behind her,
drained, for a moment, and a flame is out. This is where most players
will first lose one, by curiosity, and it is the right place: in the
open, on her own bank, with the lantern corner twenty screens away.

If they never look: fine. The rest of the watch still works at three
fires; it is simply the living walk, and the second walk is the other
one (80).


=============================================================
一更三點 - THE WATER, AND THE DROWNED
=============================================================

THE AUTHORED THING: the first creature, 水鬼, the drowned, whose
entire behaviour is one rule about her (24): it cannot leave the water
until something living takes its place.

WHERE: the east water [45] - the washing slab with the paddle someone
set down and never came back for. That paddle is the 水鬼's. She was
washing here. (Said nowhere. Shown once.)

THE RULE, as the player meets it:

  THREE FIRES (living). Lean over the slab: her reflection, three
  flames, the paddle, nothing else. Lean and stay, longer than is
  sensible - past eight seconds - and the water reaches for what is
  living: the surface breaks once, a cold touch, the SHOULDER TAP,
  and a flame is out. No figure shown. Her line, the only one:

      夜裡別對著水發呆。
      "Don't stand staring at night water."

  Taught: leaning is not free. The drowned take the living who lean.
  A player who never lingers never meets this, which is correct.

  ONE OR TWO FIRES (ambiguous). The water will not hold still - the
  reflection shivers, the flames swim. Nothing reaches; nothing
  shows. The middle is useless, by design.

  ZERO FIRES (dead). Lean: no reflection. And the drowned is there,
  in the water, plate [14], the paddle's owner, looking up at her.
  It speaks, the world's voice, once:

      你不成。你也是沒了的。
      "You're no use to me. You're gone too."

  and sinks. No cost, no threat. It wanted a substitute and she is
  not one. FIRST READING: a drowned thing sizes her up and dismisses
  her - a relief, almost, after the reach at three fires. SECOND
  READING: it told her exactly what she is, in the first watch, and
  the game moved on. This is 69's early door: a player who went to
  zero on purpose in hour one is handed the truth, and it reads as a
  monster's line.

THE MERCY, noted for later: LORE says a 水鬼 that refuses a substitute
is promoted to a water god. Somewhere after the first watch, when
she has a living thing to offer it and does not, it becomes something
else. Not here. Here it is only the rule.

Her reflection is NEEDED ART: a small ink figure-on-water plate, drawn
as her seen from above in still water, for the three water screens.
Until it exists the flames alone carry the lean, as they do now.


=============================================================
一更四點 - THE SHRINE, THE BLOCKS, AND THE STONE
=============================================================

THE AUTHORED THING: the ask verb, and the first true lock.

THE SHRINE. Inland east [57] has its niche, red cloth, a stub of
incense. Touch it: a pair of crescent blocks lies in it, plate [22].
She takes them. Her line:

    借一下，回頭還。
    "Borrowing. I'll bring them back."

From now, tapping the empty ground at her feet throws them. They land
in the painting at her feet - two blocks on stone, one of three ways:

    聖筊   one flat one curved   yes - this place leads on
    陰筊   both curved           no - not this way
    笑筊   both flat             the god is laughing. wrong question.

Authored per screen, plain data in the content table:
  聖筊 at mooring, bridge, bank east, bridge B, alley, junction,
       inland west - places the rite passes through
  陰筊 at the gate, the waters (this is not a way), bank end
  笑筊 at the house with the lamp, the neighbour's wall, the lantern
       corner, the gate lane - places where "this way?" is not the
       question. The god laughing at her own door is the best line
       in the watch and it is two blocks on a step.

No explanation of the three casts anywhere in the game. The almanac
has the entry; the blocks teach themselves in three throws.

THE STONE. The bank's far end [44] gets its 石敢當, plate [17]: the
stone that stands where a road ends and refuses passage (27). It is
the lock on the way to district two, and it is the first time the
two towns are FELT:

  THREE FIRES: the stone is a stone. The lane beyond it opens
  (district two's first screen - generated when the map is drawn;
  until then, the stone holds and the blocks say 陰筊). She passes.
  FEWER: the stone refuses. It is set against malign influence, and
  she reads as not-quite-living. Her line, at the refusal:

      石敢當。說我不乾淨。
      "Stone-dares. It's calling me unclean."

  which is the rule stated as a complaint, and on the second walk it
  is exactly true.

So the first true puzzle is the loop the whole game runs on: lose a
fire (curiosity at the dead end, or the water's reach), be refused by
the stone, remember the warm place on canal two, go back for the
warmth, return whole, pass. Nothing is told. The stone, the lantern
and the fires are all already in the district; the loop is wiring
and three lines.


=============================================================
一更五點 - THE WARM PLACE, AND THE VOICE
=============================================================

THE AUTHORED THING: the first relight, and ENDING-SITE plant 3.

THE WARM PLACE. The lantern corner relights (already built). Two
additions make it read: a brazier under the stall counter, small,
lit - the sound when Simon finds it - and her line on the first
relight, once:

    暖和。站一會兒。
    "Warm. Stand a moment."

AND THE WATCHMAN, MOVING WARMTH (84, CHAPTERS): he is a living body
with a lamp. When he crosses the mooring, standing in his path as he
passes relights one flame if she is short - "close to a living person
who does not know you are there". The player who has lost a fire and
happens to be at the mooring when he passes gets it back without
knowing why. The second walk knows why.

THE VOICE. The last minutes of the watch, wherever she is, from
behind her, distant, once:

    阿秀——

and nothing else. No sting, no cost, nothing to answer - there is no
input for answering yet, and that is the point: the player learns the
shape of the call before it ever costs. Her line:

    走路的時候沒人會叫我。規矩大家都懂。
    "No one calls me on the walk. Everyone knows the rule."

False comfort (73): someone IS calling her, and it is the person who
loves her most. Then the clapper, slow once, quick once, and the
二更 card. The first watch ends.


=============================================================
WHAT THIS FORCES ON THE BUILD
=============================================================

  1. Touch targets per screen in the content table (the door gods on
     bank east, the road money on bank end, the shrine niche, the
     stone) - the studs' mechanism, generalised to data.
  2. The lean timer at the waters, and the shoulder tap at eight
     seconds while living. The drowned's appearance at zero fires
     (plate [14] composited on the water screen at the lean).
  3. The blocks: a held-item flag, the throw on ground-tap, the cast
     plate [22] drawn at her feet, authored answers per node.
  4. The stone at bank end: plate [17] composited; passage consults
     passWard-style reading; the onward exit exists only when it
     passes and district two exists.
  5. Look back as hold (if ratified): the previous screen shown
     drained behind her for ~600ms, then the cost.
  6. The watchman as moving warmth: a relight if she is short and on
     the mooring while his walk value is mid-crossing.
  7. The voice at the watch's edge: a scripted line keyed to the
     point index, not a node.
  8. NEEDED ART: her reflection plate for the waters. Optional: a
     brazier on the lantern screen (or the sound carries it).


=============================================================
OPEN QUESTIONS - answer with numbers
=============================================================

1. LOOK BACK AS HOLD. Replace the double-tap with press-and-hold,
   showing the previous screen drained behind her before the cost?
   Recommend yes - the double-tap is undiscoverable and never felt
   like turning.

2. THE DROWNED. (a) Leaning past eight seconds at three fires costs
   a flame, no figure shown. (b) At zero fires it appears and
   dismisses her - the early door to the truth. Recommend both; (b)
   is the first watch's one moment for the player who went all the
   way down on purpose.

3. THE STONE AT THE BANK'S END as the lock on district two, passing
   the living and refusing the rest. Recommend yes - it is the whole
   game's loop met once, with every piece already in the district.

4. THE BLOCKS: taken from the shrine, thrown by tapping the ground,
   the question always "this way?", answers authored per screen, the
   god laughing at her own door. Recommend yes.

5. THE WATCHMAN AS MOVING WARMTH on his crossing. Recommend yes - it
   costs nothing and the second walk pays it.

6. THE REFLECTION PLATE - generate her reflection for the waters now
   (one prompt, three screens), or carry the lean on flames alone
   until the end-game needs it? Recommend now: the zero-fires "no
   reflection" only means something if there was one.
