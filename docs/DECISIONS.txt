THE RITUAL

6. The frame ritual is 走百病 (zou bai bing), "walking off the hundred
   illnesses." Ming-Qing, attested with regional variants. On the
   fifteenth or sixteenth night of the first lunar month, women walked
   out after dark in groups to shed the coming year's sickness.

7. Its real rules become the game's real rules:
      cross three bridges          走三桥
      touch the gate studs         摸钉   (a pun on 丁, for a son)
      do not turn back
      be home before light

8. This is the Chinese arsgang. Same shape: a real calendar night, a
   bounded walk, a prohibition list, an outcome that binds the year ahead.
   It is also genuinely obscure, which is the "found, not invented"
   quality the whole thing runs on.

9. Ghost Month / 中元 is DEAD as a frame. See 90.

SETTING

10. Late Qing. A Jiangnan water town. Canals, stone bridges, whitewash
    and black tile.

11. ONE region, ONE century, sourced. The failure mode of this genre is
    pan-Chinese soup - Journey to the West plus jiangshi plus a fox
    spirit plus a bronze mask. Everything in the game comes from Jiangnan
    or is explicitly marked as an import by a character who is wrong.

12. The water town is chosen because its geography IS the ritual. A town
    made of bridges, for a rite about crossing three of them.

THE THREE SYSTEMS

13. The game's architecture is three real folk systems, not invented
    mechanics. This is the whole bet. Each one is a rule a player can
    look up and verify.

14. SPACE = THE THREE BRIDGES. You walk left and right along a canal
    bank on a flat plane. You go DEEPER into the world only by crossing a
    bridge. Three bridges, three depth layers. The town rearranges behind
    each crossing.

    This replaces Year Walk's arbitrary tree-corridors with something
    diegetic. You are never confused about how to go deeper. You are
    confused about where deeper GOES.

15. TIME = THE FIVE WATCHES. 一更 二更 三更 四更 五更, the five
    two-hour watches of the night, roughly 7pm to 5am. Period-accurate,
    and a clock the player can feel tightening. Cockcrow at the end of
    五更 is the deadline in rule 7.

    EXTENDED BY 88 - at eight hours a watch is ninety minutes, which is
    too long to be a stopping point on a phone.

88. CHAPTERS ARE 更點, WATCHES AND POINTS. Your call, 2026-08-19. Each
    watch subdivides into five 點 of about twenty-four minutes, which
    is period-real and is also exactly a phone session.

    TWENTY-FIVE CHAPTERS, NOT FIVE. Five is the count of WATCHES, which
    are ninety-minute acts and the only thing that gets a title card.
    The chapter is the 點. 25 x 20 minutes is the eight hours at 83.

    Points are NOT uniform - real watches stretched with the season,
    and ours is the longest night of the year. Ten to thirty-five
    minutes each, averaging out to 83's target. Full scheme in
    CHAPTERS.txt.

    The strike encodes the count: watch as slow deep strikes, point as
    quick light ones. The player teaches themselves to read it inside
    the first watch, and from then on it is clock, chapter number,
    progress bar and save indicator in one, with no UI at all.

89. THE 打更人 NIGHTWATCHMAN MARKS THEM. You hear his clapper, he calls
    三更二點, the game autosaves. No menu, no card, no chapter select
    anywhere. He is simultaneously the clock, the save point, the place
    marker, a MOVING relight source per 84, and the only other person
    out walking all night.

    Free beat: early on he greets her, ordinary and bored. Hours later
    he walks straight past. No cue, no comment. Costs one deleted line
    of dialogue and confirms ending B harder than any set piece.

    He must never be written as spooky or as an omen. He is a cold man
    doing a tedious job.

    He is SILENT for the door sequence. Last call is 五更三點, minutes
    before she arrives. The silence after it is the loudest thing in
    the game.

16. LIFE = THE THREE FIRES. 三把火. Folk belief: a living person carries
    three flames, one on each shoulder and one on the crown. The dead
    cannot see you while they burn. They go out if someone taps your
    shoulder at night, if you turn your head sharply, if you answer to
    your name in the dark.

    THIS IS THE HEALTH SYSTEM AND IT IS THE BEST IDEA IN THE DOCUMENT.

17. Consequence of 16: losing all three fires is NOT a game over. It is a
    lore-accurate transformation. With no fires you are indistinguishable
    from the dead - which means the dead stop hiding from you, and half
    the map only opens in that state. The lose condition is a door.

18. Consequence of 16: "do not turn back" (rule 7) costs a fire instead of
    being forbidden. Every prohibition is a PRICE, never a wall. The
    player is always allowed to break the rite. The rite just charges.

19. Something calls your name from behind you, by name, in the dark. The
    correct play is to not touch the screen. Doing nothing is a verb.

THE ASK / THE HINT SYSTEM

20. There is no hint system. There is 擲筊 (jiaobei) - the two crescent
    divination blocks. You throw them to ask the world a yes/no question.
    Three outcomes, all real:
       one up one down   聖筊   yes
       both flat up      笑筊   the god is laughing. ask a better question.
       both curved up    陰筊   no

21. 笑筊 is the point. An unreliable oracle that tells you your QUESTION
    was wrong is worth more than a hint button.

22. Second knowledge verb: 求籤, drawing a numbered fortune stick that
    gives you a four-line poem you have to interpret yourself. The poem
    is the clue. Nobody explains it.

THE BESTIARY

25. 姑获鸟 the night-bird woman - women dead in childbirth, who take

STORY - THE ENDING AND WHAT IT COSTS
=============================================================

62. SETTLED, see 55. She died in childbirth before the game begins. The
    walk is 姑獲鳥's night. The reveal reframes every encounter: they
    were never hiding from you, they were hiding from what you are.

63. She is walking home to her baby. The child LIVED. Every maternal
    beat in the game is sincere and true, and the horror is that her
    arriving is the worst thing that can happen. Nothing about her love
    is a trick. That is what keeps this from being a cheap rug-pull.

64. CRAFT FLOOR: 姑獲鳥 is a myth about women dying in childbirth, which
    was an ordinary mass horror for most of human history. Play it as
    tragedy, not as monster-of-the-week. The bird is grief with a rule
    set. If any scene makes her death feel like a gotcha, it is wrong.

THE CONSEQUENCES - these are now load-bearing

65. EVERY ENCOUNTER MUST READ TWO WAYS. First pass: it menaces you.
    Second pass, knowing: it defers, avoids, or is afraid. No line of
    dialogue and no creature behavior ships until it survives both
    readings. This is the expensive part of the whole game and it is
    also the entire reason anyone will write about it.

66. THE THREE FIRES ARE NOT HEALTH. They are DENIAL. She is dead and
    does not know it; the fires are the performance of being alive.
    Losing one is not damage, it is the mask slipping. At zero she is
    seen for what she is, which is why the dead stop hiding. The game
    must never use the word health, never show a bar, never say you
    lost anything.

67. Consequence of 66: the fires are counted DIEGETICALLY, in the canal.
    You lean over the water and count the flames in your reflection.
    A water town is nothing but reflective surfaces, and the 水鬼 lives
    in exactly the surface you have to keep checking. Perfect convergence.

68. Consequence of 67: at zero fires, THE REFLECTION IS GONE. No text,
    no sting. You lean over the water to count and there is only water.
    That is the moment the player understands, and it should arrive
    roughly ten minutes before she does.

69. DRAMATIC IRONY, NOT A RUG PULL. Let the player get there first. The
    last stretch of the game is watching a woman you now understand walk
    happily toward the thing you cannot stop. Dread beats surprise, and
    a clever player getting it early becomes a feature instead of a
    failure.

70. 摸釘 IS NOW THE CRUELEST OBJECT IN THE GAME. She touches the gate
    studs for a son, in the first five minutes, sincerely. She already
    had him. He is the reason she is dead.

71. THE THREE BRIDGES ARE A FUNERAL ROUTE. 度厄 stops meaning "crossing
    out of misfortune" and starts meaning the procession. Reread 14
    with that in mind - the town rearranging behind each crossing is the
    road closing behind a coffin.

72. 買路錢 PAYS OFF. Road-buying money is scattered to clear a route for
    the DEAD. The prohibition against picking up money on the road has
    been in front of the player all night. It is her own road money. She
    has been walking past the payments for her own passage.

73. THE VOICE BEHIND YOU IS 叫魂. Someone living is calling her soul
    home - the husband, the mother, whoever is holding the child. It was
    never hostile. It was always someone trying to bring her back.

74. THE FINAL INPUT IS ANSWERING TO YOUR NAME. Two hours of training the
    player that responding costs a fire, that the correct play is to do
    nothing - and the last thing the game asks is whether she answers.
    This is the "enter the code" beat. The whole game is the setup.

75. Therefore 52 IS SETTLED: SHE HAS A NAME, and the game says it aloud.
    Rule 74 does not work on an unnamed avatar. Player projection loses;
    take the trade.

76. WHERE THE WALK ENDS: not at the city gate. At the water she drowned
    in, or the room she died in, or the house with the child in it -
    and the almanac has been quietly telling you which since the start.
    Pick one and plant it in 一更. OPEN, see 81.

77. THE ALMANAC IS THE MURDER WEAPON. The 姑獲鳥 entry is present, dry
    and factual and readable, from the first minute. It never occurs to
    anyone that it is a mirror. Nothing is hidden. That is the trick.

78. THE ALMANAC UPDATE AFTER COMPLETION: the entry for 正月十六 gains one
    line in its 忌 column - 忌 夜行, avoid walking at night - and her
    name beneath it. She is now an entry. Somebody else will read her
    next year.

79. THE PLAYER'S HANDS DO IT. 姑獲鳥 marks children by touching clothing
    left out overnight. At least one puzzle must require the player to
    handle laundry on a line, for a good and innocent reason, at their
    own initiative. Year Walk made you the murderer. This makes you the
    bird before you know you are.

80. REPLAYABILITY IS NOW A REQUIREMENT, NOT A BONUS. B only pays if the
    second walk is a different game. Budget for it. This is the cost of
    choosing B and it was chosen with eyes open.
