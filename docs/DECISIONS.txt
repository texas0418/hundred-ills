THE RITUAL

6. The frame ritual is 走百病 (zou bai bing), "walking off the hundred
   illnesses." Ming-Qing, attested with regional variants. On the
   fifteenth or sixteenth night of the first lunar month, women walked
   out after dark in groups to shed the coming year's sickness.

7. Its real rules become the game's real rules:
      cross three bridges          走三桥
      touch the gate studs         摸钉   (a pun on 丁, for a son)
      do not turn back
      be home before light

8. This is the Chinese arsgang. Same shape: a real calendar night, a
   bounded walk, a prohibition list, an outcome that binds the year ahead.
   It is also genuinely obscure, which is the "found, not invented"
   quality the whole thing runs on.

9. Ghost Month / 中元 is DEAD as a frame. See 90.

SETTING

10. Late Qing. A Jiangnan water town. Canals, stone bridges, whitewash
    and black tile.

11. ONE region, ONE century, sourced. The failure mode of this genre is
    pan-Chinese soup - Journey to the West plus jiangshi plus a fox
    spirit plus a bronze mask. Everything in the game comes from Jiangnan
    or is explicitly marked as an import by a character who is wrong.

12. The water town is chosen because its geography IS the ritual. A town
    made of bridges, for a rite about crossing three of them.

THE THREE SYSTEMS

13. The game's architecture is three real folk systems, not invented
    mechanics. This is the whole bet. Each one is a rule a player can
    look up and verify.

14. SPACE = THE THREE BRIDGES. You walk left and right along a canal
    bank on a flat plane. You go DEEPER into the world only by crossing a
    bridge. Three bridges, three depth layers. The town rearranges behind
    each crossing.

    This replaces Year Walk's arbitrary tree-corridors with something
    diegetic. You are never confused about how to go deeper. You are
    confused about where deeper GOES.

    AMENDED BY 100-104. The flat bank survives; the idea that the town
    is a LINE of them does not. Bridges now cross between the two banks
    of one canal, and 弄 lanes are what take you deeper. See 100.

15. TIME = THE FIVE WATCHES. 一更 二更 三更 四更 五更, the five
    two-hour watches of the night, roughly 7pm to 5am. Period-accurate,
    and a clock the player can feel tightening. Cockcrow at the end of
    五更 is the deadline in rule 7.

    EXTENDED BY 88 - at eight hours a watch is ninety minutes, which is
    too long to be a stopping point on a phone.

88. CHAPTERS ARE 更點, WATCHES AND POINTS. Your call, 2026-08-19. Each
    watch subdivides into five 點 of about twenty-four minutes, which
    is period-real and is also exactly a phone session.

    TWENTY-FIVE CHAPTERS, NOT FIVE. Five is the count of WATCHES, which
    are ninety-minute acts and the only thing that gets a title card.
    The chapter is the 點. 25 x 20 minutes is the eight hours at 83.

    Points are NOT uniform - real watches stretched with the season,
    and ours is the longest night of the year. Ten to thirty-five
    minutes each, averaging out to 83's target. Full scheme in
    CHAPTERS.txt.

    The strike encodes the count: watch as slow deep strikes, point as
    quick light ones. The player teaches themselves to read it inside
    the first watch, and from then on it is clock, chapter number,
    progress bar and save indicator in one, with no UI at all.

89. THE 打更人 NIGHTWATCHMAN MARKS THEM. You hear his clapper, he calls
    三更二點, the game autosaves. No menu, no card, no chapter select
    anywhere. He is simultaneously the clock, the save point, the place
    marker, a MOVING relight source per 84, and the only other person
    out walking all night.

    Free beat: early on he greets her, ordinary and bored. Hours later
    he walks straight past. No cue, no comment. Costs one deleted line
    of dialogue and confirms ending B harder than any set piece.

    He must never be written as spooky or as an omen. He is a cold man
    doing a tedious job.

    He is SILENT for the door sequence. Last call is 五更三點, minutes
    before she arrives. The silence after it is the loudest thing in
    the game.

16. LIFE = THE THREE FIRES. 三把火. Folk belief: a living person carries
    three flames, one on each shoulder and one on the crown. The dead
    cannot see you while they burn. They go out if someone taps your
    shoulder at night, if you turn your head sharply, if you answer to
    your name in the dark.

    THIS IS THE HEALTH SYSTEM AND IT IS THE BEST IDEA IN THE DOCUMENT.

17. Consequence of 16: losing all three fires is NOT a game over. It is a
    lore-accurate transformation. With no fires you are indistinguishable
    from the dead - which means the dead stop hiding from you, and half
    the map only opens in that state. The lose condition is a door.

18. Consequence of 16: "do not turn back" (rule 7) costs a fire instead of
    being forbidden. Every prohibition is a PRICE, never a wall. The
    player is always allowed to break the rite. The rite just charges.

19. Something calls your name from behind you, by name, in the dark. The
    correct play is to not touch the screen. Doing nothing is a verb.

THE ASK / THE HINT SYSTEM

20. There is no hint system. There is 擲筊 (jiaobei) - the two crescent
    divination blocks. You throw them to ask the world a yes/no question.
    Three outcomes, all real:
       one up one down   聖筊   yes
       both flat up      笑筊   the god is laughing. ask a better question.
       both curved up    陰筊   no

21. 笑筊 is the point. An unreliable oracle that tells you your QUESTION
    was wrong is worth more than a hint button.

22. Second knowledge verb: 求籤, drawing a numbered fortune stick that
    gives you a four-line poem you have to interpret yourself. The poem
    is the clue. Nobody explains it.

STORY - THE ENDING AND WHAT IT COSTS
=============================================================

62. SETTLED, see 55. She died in childbirth before the game begins. The
    walk is 姑獲鳥's night. The reveal reframes every encounter: they
    were never hiding from you, they were hiding from what you are.

63. She is walking home to her baby. The child LIVED. Every maternal
    beat in the game is sincere and true, and the horror is that her
    arriving is the worst thing that can happen. Nothing about her love
    is a trick. That is what keeps this from being a cheap rug-pull.

64. CRAFT FLOOR: 姑獲鳥 is a myth about women dying in childbirth, which
    was an ordinary mass horror for most of human history. Play it as
    tragedy, not as monster-of-the-week. The bird is grief with a rule
    set. If any scene makes her death feel like a gotcha, it is wrong.

THE CONSEQUENCES - these are now load-bearing

65. EVERY ENCOUNTER MUST READ TWO WAYS. First pass: it menaces you.
    Second pass, knowing: it defers, avoids, or is afraid. No line of
    dialogue and no creature behavior ships until it survives both
    readings. This is the expensive part of the whole game and it is
    also the entire reason anyone will write about it.

66. THE THREE FIRES ARE NOT HEALTH. They are DENIAL. She is dead and
    does not know it; the fires are the performance of being alive.
    Losing one is not damage, it is the mask slipping. At zero she is
    seen for what she is, which is why the dead stop hiding. The game
    must never use the word health, never show a bar, never say you
    lost anything.

67. Consequence of 66: the fires are counted DIEGETICALLY, in the canal.
    You lean over the water and count the flames in your reflection.
    A water town is nothing but reflective surfaces, and the 水鬼 lives
    in exactly the surface you have to keep checking. Perfect convergence.

68. Consequence of 67: at zero fires, THE REFLECTION IS GONE. No text,
    no sting. You lean over the water to count and there is only water.
    That is the moment the player understands, and it should arrive
    roughly ten minutes before she does.

69. DRAMATIC IRONY, NOT A RUG PULL. Let the player get there first. The
    last stretch of the game is watching a woman you now understand walk
    happily toward the thing you cannot stop. Dread beats surprise, and
    a clever player getting it early becomes a feature instead of a
    failure.

70. 摸釘 IS NOW THE CRUELEST OBJECT IN THE GAME. She touches the gate
    studs for a son, in the first five minutes, sincerely. She already
    had him. He is the reason she is dead.

71. THE THREE BRIDGES ARE A FUNERAL ROUTE. 度厄 stops meaning "crossing
    out of misfortune" and starts meaning the procession. Reread 14
    with that in mind - the town rearranging behind each crossing is the
    road closing behind a coffin.

72. 買路錢 PAYS OFF. Road-buying money is scattered to clear a route for
    the DEAD. The prohibition against picking up money on the road has
    been in front of the player all night. It is her own road money. She
    has been walking past the payments for her own passage.

73. THE VOICE BEHIND YOU IS 叫魂. Someone living is calling her soul
    home - the husband, the mother, whoever is holding the child. It was
    never hostile. It was always someone trying to bring her back.

74. THE FINAL INPUT IS ANSWERING TO YOUR NAME. Two hours of training the
    player that responding costs a fire, that the correct play is to do
    nothing - and the last thing the game asks is whether she answers.
    This is the "enter the code" beat. The whole game is the setup.

75. Therefore 52 IS SETTLED: SHE HAS A NAME, and the game says it aloud.
    Rule 74 does not work on an unnamed avatar. Player projection loses;
    take the trade.

76. WHERE THE WALK ENDS: not at the city gate. At the water she drowned
    in, or the room she died in, or the house with the child in it -
    and the almanac has been quietly telling you which since the start.
    Pick one and plant it in 一更. OPEN, see 81.

77. THE ALMANAC IS THE MURDER WEAPON. The 姑獲鳥 entry is present, dry
    and factual and readable, from the first minute. It never occurs to
    anyone that it is a mirror. Nothing is hidden. That is the trick.

78. THE ALMANAC UPDATE AFTER COMPLETION: the entry for 正月十六 gains one
    line in its 忌 column - 忌 夜行, avoid walking at night - and her

79. THE PLAYER'S HANDS DO IT. 姑獲鳥 marks children by touching clothing

100. THE DESIGN CONTRADICTED ITSELF AND SIMON CAUGHT IT.

     33 promises that players will draw maps on paper, and calls that
     the loudest signal the game respects them. 14 specified a flat
     bank with three bridges on it.

     A LINE NEEDS NO MAP. Both could not be true, and the code had
     built the line - an endless left-to-right treadmill.

     Resolved in favour of 33. The walking stays horizontal, but the
     town is a NETWORK of horizontal strips, and the player may wander
     it freely.

101. THE THREE ELEMENTS. All of them are things a Jiangnan water town
     is actually made of, so none of this is invented level design.

     岸  BANK   a horizontal strip you walk along. Every canal has two
                of them, facing each other across the water. This is
                what is already built.
     橋  BRIDGE a landmark ON her path, not a way off it. CORRECTED BY
                105 - the first version of this line had bridges
                crossing to the far bank, which a flat side-on view
                cannot draw.
     弄  LANE   a narrow alley running away from the water. THE ONLY
                BRANCHING IN THE GAME. A gap in the wall you turn into.

     Movement never leaves the horizontal. A lane or a bridge is a
     transition between strips, not a new axis to steer on. The player
     is always walking left or right; the choice is which strip they
     come out on.

102. 走三桥 IS THE CONSTRAINT, AND IT IS THE PUZZLE.

     The rite requires three bridges, and per LORE.txt some accounts
     require three DIFFERENT bridges with no doubling back. So:

       The town is open. The rite through it is not.
       Any bridge may be crossed. Only three of them count.
       They must be three distinct bridges.
       Recrossing one you have already used is doubling back.

     ENFORCED BY THE FIRES, NOT BY A LOCKED GATE. Recrossing a spent
     bridge is turning back, and turning back costs a flame - exactly
     the price already set at 18, where every prohibition is a price
     and never a wall. Wander as much as you like; wandering carelessly
     makes you less alive.

     THE GAME NEVER TELLS YOU WHICH BRIDGES YOU HAVE SPENT. That is
     what the paper is for. 33 and 41 both hold.

103. SCALE, per district, and these are starting numbers not gospel:
     two or three canals, four to six banks, three to five bridges,
     two to four lanes. Small enough to learn, tangled enough that
     learning it takes a pencil.

105. BRIDGES ARE LANDMARKS, LANES ARE THE BRANCHING.
     Corrected 2026-08-20, same day, after Simon looked at a bridge on
     the phone and asked why one was standing in the middle of a
     walkway. He was right and the reason is geometric.

     A FLAT SIDE-ON VIEW CANNOT SHOW A CROSSING TOWARD THE CAMERA. She
     walks along a bank looking across the canal. A bridge over THAT
     canal comes toward the viewer and would have to be foreshortened,
     which the whole art direction forbids. What can honestly be drawn
     side-on is a bridge over a SIDE CANAL - a smaller waterway joining
     the main one, where the lane humps up and over it.

     So a bridge is something she walks OVER, on her way along the
     bank. It is scenery and it is ritual. It is not a way to somewhere
     else.

     THE ONLY WAY TO ANOTHER STRIP IS A 弄 LANE - an opening in the
     wall she turns into. That also happens to be the one transition a
     flat view can draw honestly, because a gap needs no perspective.

     走三桥 IS UNHARMED. The rite says cross three bridges. It never
     said they change which side of anything you are on. Three
     side-canal bridges walked over is a complete 走三桥, and the
     no-doubling-back rule at 102 works exactly as written.

     ART CONSEQUENCE: the existing bridge plates are LANDMARK views -
     a bridge seen across water, with its reflection. Those stay, for
     seeing a bridge at a distance. A bridge she walks over needs a
     different picture: the parapet beside her at lane level, the deck
     humping up and down, no reflection and no full arch.

106. WHAT THIS MAKES NECESSARY RATHER THAN DECORATIVE.

     Three systems were sitting in the design doing nothing, because a
     corridor gives them nothing to do:

       石敢當 blocks a lane - which only means something when there is
              another lane, and which is now the single most important
              obstacle in the game, because lanes are the only branching.
       門神   gate houses - which only matters when there is a choice
              of door.
       THE FIRE STATE opens and closes different routes, per 84 - so
              the same network is two networks, and the map the player
              drew while alive is wrong once she is not.

     And the third bridge, the covered one where she sees the body, is
     now a place to FIND rather than a gate to arrive at.


107. THE WALKWAY IS AUTHORED SCENES, NOT TILES PLUS OBJECTS.
     Decided 2026-08-21, after the bridge failed four times in one
     night and Simon called for a restart. Scope: the COMPOSITION
     approach only. Engine, docs, style and most plates survive.

     WHAT DIED, and why it stays dead: compositing self-contained AI
     paintings onto tiling planes at runtime. Four failures, one
     disease in different clothes -
       alignment constants that describe one image, not a class
       opacity synthesised from luminance, fighting the art
       tiling phase mismatches wherever an overlay met the plane
     Year Walk never did this. Simogo authored whole scenes; the
     bridge was painted INTO its landscape by the artist, and seams
     existed only at fixed scene boundaries.

     THE NEW MODEL, scoped to the MID PLANE (the walkway - the only
     plane objects ever stood on):
       A strip's walkway is a SEQUENCE OF AUTHORED SEGMENTS drawn at
       fixed world positions. A bridge is not an object placed on a
       segment; it is a segment WITH A BRIDGE IN IT, painted as one
       scene by the model. North bank = three segments of 1200 world
       px: plain, bridge-a, bridge-b.
       Segments join edge to edge under the tile-cut rule that already
       works: embankment cut at both edges at the same height.

     WHAT SURVIVES UNCHANGED: far plane and kerb keep tiling - they are
     deliberately featureless and have never had a seam problem. The
     lane mouth stays a far-plane overlay; pale-on-pale, it composites
     cleanly and looked right on the device. Parallax, the drain, the
     fires, the watches: untouched.

     GAMEPLAY DATA FOLLOWS THE ART: a bridge's world x is wherever the
     segment's painting put it, measured after the art lands - never
     the other way round.

     The runtime machinery this kills (WorldObject bridge drawing, the
     OPAQUE/SEAL alpha special-casing) comes out when the first bridge
     segment lands.

108. THE TOWN IS A DEPTH FIELD. FORWARD IS A VERB.
     Simon, 2026-08-21, choosing the fuller Year Walk model over
     doorway-only transitions. Amends 101 and 105.

     Year Walk's forward motion is the fact this is built on: swiping
     into the screen moves between DEPTH LAYERS, and every layer is
     still flat, side-on art. Nothing recedes. The layers part like
     stage flats. So this changes traversal and feel, and does not
     change one rule of the art doctrine.

     THE MODEL:
       The town is a stack of parallel strips, ordered by depth:
       water's edge, bank, back lane, inner yards, the next canal's
       bank, and so on. Swipe UP steps deeper (inland, away from the
       water). Swipe DOWN steps outward (back toward the water).

       Stepping is possible in PASSAGE ZONES - stretches where the art
       shows a way through: a gap in the terrace, a break in a wall.
       More generous than the single lane mouth of 105, but not
       everywhere; a town has walls. Where the swipe is refused she
       leans toward the gap that is not there, and nothing else
       happens. 石敢當 blocks a zone - its whole job, per 104/106.

     THE UNIFICATION, and the best consequence of the change:
       On the outermost strip - the bank - stepping DOWN means going to
       the water itself, and that IS the reflection. Counting her
       flames stops being a separate gesture and becomes the last
       outward step of the depth verb. One verb: toward the water or
       away from it. The rite walks the line between.

     WHAT THIS COSTS, stated rather than discovered later:
       The lane-mouth as sole legible branch point is gone; legibility
       must now come from the art of each passage zone.
       Every inland layer needs strip art. The demo pair (bank +
       back lane) exists; a second canal does not, yet.
       The transition must be BUILT AND FELT - layers sliding apart,
       Year Walk fashion - or the whole change is a teleport with
       extra steps. This is engine work and it is the next milestone.

     走三桥 IS UNTOUCHED. Bridges stay landmarks on bank strips,
     crossed by walking past, per 105/107.

     101's "movement never leaves the horizontal" is amended: her BODY
     still only walks left and right along each strip; the depth step
     is a discrete transition between strips, never a steerable axis.
     The art stays flat forever.

109. THE WORLD IS DISCRETE SCREENS. NOTHING TILES, EVER.
     Simon, 2026-08-21, after playing the fires loop: "none of it is
     fun, engaging, beautiful - it feels pieced together and you can
     tell where there are seams and pieces missing." He is right, and
     his diagnosis of Year Walk is the fix: you move, and the view
     PHASES to a new screen. It looks like movement without piecing
     scenes together or repeating a drawing.

     THE UNIT OF ART IS THE SCREEN. Each place in the town is ONE
     complete, full-frame painting - bank, water, mist, reflections,
     bridge, all composed together in a single generation, judged as a
     picture someone would hang. Screens never abut on-screen, so there
     is nothing to align and nothing to seam. Nothing repeats because
     every screen is unique art.

     TRAVERSAL IS THE TOWN GRAPH, NODE BY NODE. A screen has exits -
     left, right, inward, outward - which are the depth field of 108
     made discrete. Swiping phases to the neighbouring screen: the
     leaving screen parts and the arriving one settles, the layers-part
     choreography reused at screen scale. Within a screen there is no
     scrolling and no walking; the screen IS the place.

     WHAT DIES: tiling, pairing, walkway band alignment, segments.json,
     slot rendering, the continuous drag-walk and its momentum. The
     smooth drag was praised and is knowingly given up - it was smooth
     motion through assembled art, and assembled is the thing that
     failed. What is felt now is the phase between authored pictures.

     WHAT SURVIVES UNTOUCHED: every pure system. Fires, warmth,
     watches and points, 走三桥 accounting (bridge screens spend the
     rite as you pass through them), wards, the drain (a cross-fade of
     whole screens now, which will look BETTER), the depth verb as exit
     directions, the town graph itself - a node map is what 100-104
     always described, and players drawing maps of nodes is the 33
     promise at full strength.

     SCOPE HONESTY: every screen is bespoke art. The demo bank becomes
     roughly six to eight screens instead of one strip plus plates. The
     game total is on the order of a hundred-plus screens across five
     watches - which is exactly Year Walk's shape, and the reason its
     two hours feel dense.

     PROOF BEFORE MIGRATION: two screens and one phase transition on
     the device, judged by Simon, before any machinery is deleted.

     PROOF ACCEPTED 2026-08-22. Three screens, four-way phase, on the
     device. Simon: "that's more like it - this is more what I remember
     and want", then "it works well and it looks great." The discrete-
     screen model is the game's architecture from here.

     RULE LEARNED FROM THE PROOF, and it is binding on every future
     screen: AN EXIT MUST BE VISIBLE IN THE PAINTING. Simon could not
     find the third screen because nothing in the bridge painting
     showed that forward existed. A gap, a passage, steps, a bridge
     deck - the art declares the exits, or the node does not get one.


110. SHE FACES INLAND AND NEVER TURNS. THE PAINTING'S PATH IS THE
     SWIPE. Ratified by Simon 2026-08-21 after walking b54 with all
     nineteen screens of the first district in hand.

     The exit rule at 109 caught MISSING exits. It never said which
     way an exit runs, and three screens proved the gap: the far
     bank's lane ran corner to corner into the distance while its
     return was wired sideways; the gate lane, reached by stepping
     back from the gate, had no gate in it; the mooring's "right to
     the bridge" had no rightward bank drawn at all. Every screen was
     beautiful alone and the town did not hold together in the hand.

     THE RULE, in four parts, binding on every screen from here:

     FACING. She faces inland and keeps facing inland. Swipe up is a
     step deeper into the picture; swipe down is a step back out of
     it, still looking the same way. She does not turn. Left and
     right walk along a bank or lane that runs ACROSS the frame,
     with the water beside her.

     THE PATH IS THE SWIPE. A lane drawn running left-right across
     the frame is a left/right pair. A lane drawn from the foreground
     into the distance is an up/down pair. Never wire across that;
     if the painting's geometry and the graph disagree, the painting
     is regenerated with the direction in the prompt, or the edge
     goes.

     ARRIVALS. The place she came from is in frame at the edge she
     entered from: the gate small at the lane's far end, the end of
     the bridge at the bank's edge, the house at the end of the
     neighbour's wall. A screen shows its exits AND its arrivals.

     FORKS. Two ways ahead cannot be a left/right swipe - both are
     forward. A fork is chosen by TOUCH: the player taps the mouth
     they want, the ambiguous swipe refuses. The world answers
     touches; it already did at the studs.

     HER FEET ARE THE BOTTOM EDGE. Added 2026-08-23 after Simon walked
     b59: the bottom edge of every painting is where she stands. On a
     bank that edge is water, so "back" from a bank is impossible and
     the wiring that sent it to the water's edge was lying - three
     times, at every water's edge in the district. The water's edge is
     not a step; it is the LEAN of 67, she bends over the canal to
     count her flames. So it is reached by TOUCHING the water, the
     swipe down refuses, and up straightens her back onto the bank.
     The alley landing [58] was the same disease seen once: water
     behind her where the bridge should be.

     COST ON THE FIRST DISTRICT: five rerolls with direction-aware
     prompts (gate lane, mooring, bank east, inland west, inland
     east), two rewires (bank end becomes a depth dead end past
     bridge B; the junction becomes a touch fork), and [40] the far
     bank BENCHED until a far-bank lateral slot exists. The prompt
     kinds "depth" and "lateral" exist now so the direction is in
     every future ask and the perspective negatives stop fighting it.
