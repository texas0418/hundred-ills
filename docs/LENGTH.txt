HUNDRED ILLS - LENGTH
Compiled 2026-08-19. Supersedes DECISIONS 32 and 43.

DIRECTIVE: it needs to be hours long.
TARGET SET: 6-8 hours for the first walk. 10-12 including the second.


=============================================================
THE ONLY REAL DANGER
=============================================================

Year Walk is two hours because Simogo cut everything. Going to eight
means SIX MORE HOURS OF AUTHORED CONTENT. It does not mean six hours
of walking, a bigger town, or slower doors.

Every hour added has to come from one of the sources below. If an hour
cannot be traced to one of them, it is padding and it will be the thing
reviews mention.


=============================================================
SOURCE ONE - THE FIRES BECOME THE TRAVERSAL SYSTEM
=============================================================

This is the answer. Everything else is secondary.

Right now the three fires only go DOWN, which makes them a countdown.
Let them go up as well and the game doubles without a single new
street.

  RELIGHTING. 陽氣, yang vitality, is warmth and life and fire. You
  relight a flame at a hearth, a brazier, a lamp, an oven, or by
  standing close to a living person who does not know you are there.
  All of these are sourced, all are ordinary objects in a winter town.

  EXTINGUISHING is already designed. Turn and look behind you. Answer
  to your name. Let something tap your shoulder. Per DECISIONS 18,
  these were always prices rather than walls. Now the player pays them
  ON PURPOSE.

THE TWO TOWNS, ON THE SAME GEOGRAPHY:

  THREE FIRES - the living town. You read as alive, so the door gods
  let you pass, per DECISIONS 60. Warm houses, hearths, people behind
  the doors. Registration perfect. But the dead hide from the living,
  so the entire spirit layer is invisible and unreachable.

  ZERO FIRES - the dead town. The door gods see what you are and every
  warded house is shut to you. But the dead stop hiding: the spirit
  layer is visible and walkable, and the places with lapsed or broken
  wards - the ruined shrine, the water, the covered bridge - are open.
  No reflection. The colour plates have walked off the drawing.

  CORRECTED 2026-08-19. An earlier draft of this passage had the door
  gods backwards. Wards are installed against the DEAD. Alive gets you
  into houses, dead gets you into the spirit layer. Each state still
  opens exactly what the other closes; it just now agrees with 60.

Each state opens exactly what the other closes. The player's core verb
stops being WALK and becomes DECIDE HOW ALIVE TO BE.

WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT ANSWER AND NOT A TRICK:

  It costs almost no new art. Per ART.txt, the registration mechanic
  already renders both states from ONE set of plates. Two towns, one
  art budget.

  It is entirely sourced. Nothing above is invented. It is the three
  fires belief plus the door god custom, used honestly.

  It pays off ending B better than anything else in the document. The
  mechanic the player masters over eight hours is PRETENDING TO BE
  ALIVE. Then the game tells them she never was. The skill was the
  symptom.


=============================================================
SOURCE TWO - THREE DISTRICTS, NOT THREE SCREENS
=============================================================

DECISIONS 14 fixes three bridges. Keep it. Make each crossing open a
DISTRICT rather than a corridor - a small non-linear area you re-cross
repeatedly as your fire state changes, roughly two hours each.

  first district    the living town, learning the rules
  second district   both states in play, the real puzzles
  third district    across the covered bridge, mostly dead town

Non-linear re-traversal of a small area is the honest way to get hours.
A long corridor is the dishonest way.


=============================================================
SOURCE THREE - THE ALMANAC AS ACTUAL RESEARCH
=============================================================

Cheapest hours in the project and the ones you are best at. You have
shipped puzzle books. This is that skill, applied.

Make the 黃曆 genuinely dense - real 宜 and 忌 for every day, the
clashing zodiac and its direction, the presiding spirit, the lucky
hours - and build puzzles that require CROSS-REFERENCING it rather
than reading one entry. Which hour is safe. Which direction to leave
by. Which day a thing must have happened on.

This is hours of deduction, it is all text, it needs no art, and it is
the part of the game that makes people feel clever. It also loads the
gun for DECISIONS 77, where the almanac turns out to be about her.


=============================================================
SOURCE FOUR - HARD PUZZLES, HELD TO YOUR EXISTING DOCTRINE
=============================================================

Last Seen already runs a hard-puzzle doctrine with an anti-leak test.
Apply it here unchanged. No hints, no nudges, no waypoint. Difficulty
is legitimate length; ambiguity is not. A puzzle that takes forty
minutes because it is hard is content. One that takes forty minutes
because the player did not know it existed is a bug.


=============================================================
SOURCE FIVE - MORE CREATURES, SAME RESTRAINT
=============================================================

DECISIONS 29 capped it at four to six. Raise to EIGHT to TEN. The cap
was there to prevent dilution, and the thing that actually dilutes is
unsourced creatures, not the count. Every addition needs an [A] or [B]
entry in LORE.txt and one clear obligation it is bound by. If it
cannot be sourced, it does not go in, and we stay at six.


=============================================================
WHAT THIS COSTS - straight
=============================================================

1. ART VOLUME roughly triples, to maybe 120-180 plates. In money that
   is now fine. In CONSISTENCY it is the real risk - sixty plates that
   match is hard, a hundred and eighty is much harder, and drift will
   be invisible to you as it happens because you will see them one at
   a time. Build a contact sheet from day one and look at all of them
   together every week.

2. PLAYTESTING scales linearly and you have no testers. An eight-hour
   game with no hints and no map WILL have a wall you cannot see,
   because you know every answer. This is now the largest unmitigated
   risk in the project. It was survivable at two hours. It is not at
   eight.

3. PRICE moves. Two hours justified 3.99. Eight to twelve justifies
   7.99 to 9.99. Amend DECISIONS 53.

4. SCHEDULE. Already handled - DECISIONS 56 says ships when done.


=============================================================
THE TEST
=============================================================

For every hour of the finished game, name the authored thing in it: a
puzzle, a creature with a rule, a district in a state you have not seen
it in, or a piece of the almanac that pays off.

If an hour has no name, cut it.
