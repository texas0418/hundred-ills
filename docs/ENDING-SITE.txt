HUNDRED ILLS - WHERE THE WALK ENDS (DECISIONS 81)
Compiled 2026-08-19.

THE QUESTION
Ending B says she died in childbirth, the child lived, and she is
walking home to him. Home to WHERE, physically. It blocks writing 一更
because whatever the answer is has to be planted in the first watch.


=============================================================
FIRST - A PIECE OF LORE THAT CHANGES THE QUESTION
=============================================================

血湖地獄 / 血盆經 - the Blood Pool Hell and the Blood Bowl Sutra
[A, verify with sources - I am confident this exists and is well
studied, I have not read the scholarship with this project in mind]

A Chinese popular-religious belief, carried by an apocryphal sutra:
women who die in childbirth are consigned to a hell of blood, polluted
by the blood of the birth itself. They cannot leave. The family must
perform a releasing ritual - 破血湖, breaking the blood pool - to get
her out. In many accounts the rite is properly performed BY HER
CHILDREN, as filial duty.

Read that against our story.

  She cannot rest because the rite was never performed for her.
  The only person who can perform it is her son.
  Her son is eleven days old.

This gives her a reason to walk that is sourced rather than sentimental,
and it collapses the entire game into one action. She needs him. She
loves him. 姑獲鳥 takes children. Going to the baby is simultaneously
the mother's love, the ghost's only hope of release, and the monster's
appetite. They are not three motives in tension. They are one motion.

That is the game. Everything below is just choosing the address.


=============================================================
THE OPTIONS
=============================================================

A. THE HOUSE WITH THE CHILD IN IT

   She arrives home. The lamp is lit. The 叫魂 voice that has been
   calling her name all night is coming from inside her own house - the
   husband, or her mother, calling her soul back, the way you call a
   startled soul home. The last thing the game asks is whether she
   answers.

   FOR: it is the only option where the final input at DECISIONS 74
   lands on the child. Two hours of "never answer to your name" and the
   voice turns out to be her family trying to bring her home, and
   answering it means going in, and she is the bird. Maximum payoff,
   every system firing at once.
   AGAINST: none structural. It is the obvious answer and it is
   obvious because it is right.

B. THE ROOM SHE DIED IN

   The birthing room, preserved or stripped. She has to recognise it.

   FOR: quieter, more interior, horror by recognition rather than
   threat. Cheaper to build - one room, one reveal.
   AGAINST: puts the child offstage at the climax and wastes the
   strongest material in the game. Also largely redundant: in a real
   house the birthing room and the house with the child are the same
   building. This is not a separate ending, it is a ROOM INSIDE A.

C. THE WATER

   Her body is still in the canal, never found. That is why she cannot
   rest, and why she has spent all night leaning over water.

   FOR: it converges beautifully with the reflection mechanic at
   DECISIONS 67. Every time the player leans over the canal to count
   her fires, she is leaning over her own corpse. That is worth having
   and it is nearly free.
   AGAINST as a TERMINUS: she died in childbirth, not drowning. Adding
   a second death muddies the 姑獲鳥 claim, which is specifically about
   dying in the birth. It also steps on 水鬼's territory, and we have a
   drowned ghost already.
   VERDICT: keep the body in the water, do not end there. See the
   recommendation.

D. THE TEMPLE WHERE THE RITE WAS NEVER PERFORMED

   The 破血湖 was owed and never done. She ends at the shrine, in front
   of the god who could have released her and did not, because nobody
   paid for the ritual.

   FOR: the angriest version, and the most political - a woman failed
   by her family and by an institution, not by fate. Pairs viciously
   with 五通神 at DECISIONS 28, the local god who is not good.
   AGAINST: it is an ending about an injustice rather than about a
   child, and it asks the player to have absorbed a fairly technical
   piece of religious practice for the climax to land.
   VERDICT: this is the MERCY PATH, not the main ending. See below.

E. THE CITY GATE - CLOSED LOOP

   She ends where she began, touching the studs for a son, and the walk
   starts again. She does this every year.

   FOR: bleak, elegant, and it pays off DECISIONS 78 exactly - she
   becomes an entry in the almanac and somebody reads her next year.
   AGAINST: denies the reunion the whole game is walking toward, and
   trades a devastating specific ending for a tidy structural one.
   VERDICT: not the ending. Possibly the last four seconds after it.


=============================================================
RECOMMENDATION
=============================================================

Do not pick one. They are a sequence, and the geography already exists.

  THIRD BRIDGE  = C. The covered bridge, the only one you cannot see
                  through. She looks down at the water and there is a
                  body in it. The reflection mechanic pays off here,
                  not at the end, and this is the DECISIONS 68 moment
                  where the player understands.

  LAST STRETCH  = the walk from the water to the house, knowing. This
                  is the dramatic irony at DECISIONS 69 made literal.
                  Nothing threatens her. The player just watches her
                  hurry.

  TERMINUS      = A. The house, the lamp, the voice calling her name,
                  and one input.

  MERCY PATH    = D, hidden. If the player has done the right things
                  with the 破血湖 material - and the almanac has been
                  quietly carrying the instructions the whole time,
                  per DECISIONS 77 - the temple offers the release the
                  family never bought her. It costs her the child.
                  A second ending nobody gets on the first walk, which
                  is what DECISIONS 80 needs.

  CODA          = E, four seconds. The gate. Next year.


=============================================================
WHAT THIS FORCES INTO 一更
=============================================================

Plant in the first watch, all of it quiet and none of it flagged:

  1. The house. She passes it early and does not go in, and gives a
     mundane reason.
  2. The lamp in its window, lit. It is lit all night, every scene it
     is visible. Nobody remarks on it.
  3. The voice, once, distant, at the very edge of the first watch.
  4. The auspicious baby print on a neighbour's wall, seen plainly and
     warmly, with no music cue whatsoever.
  5. The almanac's 姑獲鳥 entry available from minute one, and the
     血湖 entry beside it, both dry, both unremarked.
  6. Her name, spoken by someone living, so the player learns it in
     the ordinary way before it is ever used against her.
