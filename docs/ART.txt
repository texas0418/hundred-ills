HUNDRED ILLS - ART DIRECTION
Compiled 2026-08-19. Supersedes "cheap picture" in DECISIONS 42.

Confidence marks as in LORE.txt: [A] attested, [B] widely held / thinly
documented, [C] my invention.


=============================================================
THE TRAP
=============================================================

The default answer for a Chinese-themed game is 水墨 ink wash. Grey
mountains, bleeding brushstrokes, a lot of negative space. It is the
visual form of the pan-Chinese soup banned at DECISIONS 11. It is what
everyone does, it is not regional, it is not period, and it is not ours.

We are not doing ink wash.


=============================================================
THE ANSWER: 桃花塢年畫
=============================================================

Taohuawu nianhua - the New Year woodblock prints of SUZHOU.  [A]

Nianhua are the prints pasted up at New Year: door gods, the Kitchen
God, fat auspicious babies holding carp, blessings for sons. Taohuawu
is the Suzhou school, which puts it in Jiangnan, in our exact region,
satisfying DECISIONS 11 with no stretching at all.

Five reasons this is not merely apt but structural.

1. THE GAME TAKES PLACE INSIDE THE OCCASION. Our night is the sixteenth
   of the first lunar month. Nianhua are the images that go up for
   precisely that new year. The art form and the calendar night are the
   same event. The pictures are not a style laid over the world - they
   are physically on the walls of the world.

2. THE SUBJECT MATTER IS THE STORY. The single most reproduced nianhua
   subject is a fat healthy baby, often with a carp, meaning 年年有餘
   and, plainly, sons. Our game is about a woman touching gate studs for
   a son and walking home to a child she died having. Rendering it in
   the print tradition whose most famous image is an auspicious baby
   means the happiest object in Chinese popular art becomes the most
   frightening object in the game. We do not have to do anything to it.
   We just have to keep showing it.

3. IT LICENSES THE DEPTH. Taohuawu at its 18th-century height - the
   姑蘇版 Gusu prints - absorbed European perspective and copperplate
   hatching through Jesuit contact, sometimes labelled 泰西筆法, western
   brush method. [A, verify wording] A print tradition that already did
   receding planes is the historical justification for our layered
   parallax walk at DECISIONS 14. The art history and the mechanic agree.

4. THE PERIOD DEGRADATION IS FREE ATMOSPHERE. Suzhou's printing quarter
   was largely destroyed during the Taiping war in the 1860s and the
   craft never recovered its former refinement. [A, verify] Late Qing
   nianhua are coarser, cheaper, fewer blocks, cruder registration than
   the Qianlong-era work. Our game is set in that afterward. The art is
   a diminished version of something that used to be finer, which is
   also what the town is, and what she is.

5. IT IS CHEAP TO PRODUCE AND EXPENSIVE TO LOOK AT. Woodblock is flat
   colour, a black keyline block, and four to six registered colour
   blocks on buff paper. No rendering, no lighting, no gradients. Two
   people can make it look like a museum piece.


=============================================================
THE MECHANIC HIDING IN THE STYLE: MISREGISTRATION
=============================================================

套色 multi-block colour printing needs the paper to line up under each
block. When it slips, the colour sits off its outline. Everyone has seen
this on a cheap print and nobody can name it, which is exactly the
register we want.  [A as a printing fact, C as a game mechanic]

WE DRIVE REGISTRATION OFF THE THREE FIRES.

  three fires   perfect registration. colour sits inside the line.
  two fires     a hair of drift. the reader feels it before they see it.
  one fire      colour visibly detached, a red ghost beside every edge.
  zero fires    the colour plates and the line plate have separated
                completely, and the coloured version of the world is
                standing slightly to one side of the drawn one.

This is the health bar rendered as a printing defect. It never says a
number, never draws a bar, and satisfies DECISIONS 66. It is also, as
far as I know, original - I have not seen a game use print registration
as a state meter.


=============================================================
THE SECOND MECHANIC: 門神
=============================================================

Door gods.  [A]  Paired warrior figures - 神荼 and 鬱壘, or 秦叔寶 and
尉遲恭 - printed as nianhua and pasted on the two leaves of a door at
New Year to keep out what should stay out.

Every door in a Jiangnan town on the sixteenth night has a pair, put up
eleven days ago.

RULE: a door with intact door gods cannot be entered. A door whose gods
are torn, faded, peeled, or pasted crooked can. The player will read
this all night as "the neglected houses are the ones open to me" and
will feel clever.

The second reading, per DECISIONS 65: they are not neglected houses.
They are houses whose protection has lapsed, and the thing the
protection lapsed against is her.

This costs us nothing. It is a locked-door system rendered as wall art,
sourced, period-correct, and it flips.


=============================================================
PALETTE
=============================================================

A New Year print, seen at night, by lamp.

  paper         buff / raw ochre, warm, grain visible at all times
  keyline       soot black, slightly broken, never pure #000
  standing ink  ONE mineral green and ONE gamboge yellow, both drained
                heavily by darkness, present but nearly given up

  RED           桃紅 peach-red / vermilion. RESERVED.

Red is the New Year colour, the door god colour, the wedding colour, the
child's-jacket colour, and the other thing. It is the only saturated
value in the game. Spend it deliberately - a couplet on a door, a scrap
of cloth, the baby's coat in a print - and let the player build the
association themselves over two hours. By the end red should be doing
work no line of dialogue could.

At zero fires, per the misregistration rule, red is the plate that has
walked away from the drawing.


=============================================================
PRODUCTION - HOW THIS GETS MADE FOR REAL
=============================================================

"Original and beautiful" and the under-500-per-bet rule are in tension.
A two-hour walk needs on the order of 30-60 scene plates plus five
creatures plus the almanac interiors. Commissioning that as finished
illustration is thousands of dollars and months.

The style choice is what makes it affordable. THE ILLUSTRATOR DRAWS LINE
ART ONLY.

  Commissioned, by a human, original:
     the keyline block for each plate
     flat colour separations as solid shapes, no shading
     the five creatures
     the auspicious-baby print, which must be genuinely lovely

  Generated in Skia at runtime, by us, free and infinitely variable:
     paper grain and fibre
     ink absorption and bleed at the edges of every shape
     registration offset per plate, driven by the fires
     lamp falloff and the darkness drain on the palette
     parallax separation of the planes
     wear, foxing, damp, and the tear state of every door god

That split is the whole production plan. The human makes the drawing.
The engine makes the printing. It cuts commissioned volume by more than
half, every plate is alive rather than static, and the same line art
renders differently at three fires and at zero, which means the second
playthrough required by DECISIONS 80 literally looks different without
drawing it twice.

RESOLVED 2026-08-19: the images are AI-GENERATED, directed by Simon.
My earlier line barring generated art is withdrawn - it was my call to
make as a recommendation, not as a rule, and it is his project.

THE SPLIT SURVIVES INTACT AND MATTERS MORE NOW.

Generate the PLATE, not the picture. The model produces flat woodblock
line-and-colour with nothing baked in. Skia still does paper, ink bleed,
registration drift, lamp, parallax and wear at runtime.

This is not aesthetic preference, it is a hard requirement of the
registration mechanic at 59. If the model bakes lighting, gradients,
texture or atmosphere into the image, the line and the colour cannot be
pulled apart, and the fires meter is dead on arrival.

HOW THE SEPARATION ACTUALLY WORKS - the reason the prompts are strict:

A true woodblock image is black keylines plus flat unmodulated colour.
That is separable by luminance in one pass. We do not need the model to
output registered plates, which it cannot do reliably. We need one image
that DECOMPOSES:

   line plate    = pixels below a luminance threshold, near-neutral
   colour plate  = everything else, flattened to its nearest palette entry
   offset the two by a few pixels and you have misregistration

A gradient anywhere in the image ruins this. A soft shadow ruins this.
Bloom ruins this. Hence the negative list in PROMPTS.txt, which is not
a style preference and must not be relaxed for a plate that "looks
better" with shading. It will look better and it will break the game.

THE THREE REAL RISKS, all practical:

1. CONSISTENCY across 30-60 plates is the hard problem, not quality.
   Solved by a locked style preamble reused verbatim, a style-reference
   image, and fixed seeds. See PROMPTS.txt.

2. THE MODEL'S DEFAULT CHINESE IS THE BANNED ONE. Ask for anything
   Chinese and you get ink wash, red lanterns, cherry blossom, gold
   dragons. Every prompt has to actively fight this, every time.

3. GENERATED CHINESE CHARACTERS ARE ALWAYS WRONG. No text in any
   generated image, ever. All characters are set live in the app with
   a real font. This is non-negotiable - the game's whole claim is that
   the reference layer is real, and a garbled couplet on a door kills it
   for exactly the audience most likely to write about the game.
