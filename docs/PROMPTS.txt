HUNDRED ILLS - IMAGE PROMPTS
Rewritten 2026-08-19 for the INK medium. The woodblock version is dead;
see DECISIONS 58. Plain text so you can copy straight out.

Read the RULES once. After that the prompts are copy-paste.


=============================================================
RULES - the five that actually matter
=============================================================

1. EVERY prompt is STYLE PREAMBLE + the plate description + NEGATIVE
   LIST, with the STYLE REFERENCE IMAGE attached. The preamble is copied
   verbatim every time, never paraphrased. Consistency across a hundred
   and fifty plates comes from the preamble being byte-identical, not
   from the model remembering.

2. THE STYLE REFERENCE IS "images (1).jpeg" - the arched stone bridge
   with the red maple. Attach it to every generation. Rename it to
   STYLE-KEY.jpg and keep it forever. It was chosen because its bridge
   is our bridge; its red canopy is NOT what we want, see rule 4.

3. 留白 IS NOT OPTIONAL. At least a quarter of every plate is bare
   paper standing for mist, water and sky. This is the single measurable
   thing that separates a Chinese painting from a generic "oriental"
   render - two of the six candidate references were rejected purely on
   having 0.8% and 3.9% white. If a plate comes back painted to all four
   edges, it is a reject however pretty it is.

4. RED IS A SLIVER, NOT A MASS. One small area of vermilion and no
   other saturated colour anywhere. The reference has red across 27% of
   its frame; we want roughly 3%. A door couplet, a scrap of cloth, one
   lantern. Never a whole canopy of red maple. Red is being saved for
   the last twenty minutes of the game and it does not work if the
   player has been soaking in it for eight hours.

5. NOT JAPAN. The single most damaging drift available. No vermilion
   lacquer bridges, no torii, no gold leaf, no stone lanterns, no
   pagodas, no cherry blossom, no koi ponds, no raked gravel. If one
   Japanese cue gets into a reference it propagates into every plate
   after it.

AND: NO TEXT. No characters, no couplet writing, no seals, no
signature. Models produce garbage characters and the one thing this
game cannot survive is a nonsense inscription. All type is set live in
the app.


=============================================================
THE STYLE PREAMBLE - copy verbatim, every time
=============================================================

Chinese ink and colour painting on xuan paper, 水墨設色, in the manner
of a late Qing Jiangnan water town. Brush and wash: soft graded ink,
wet blooming edges, visible brush direction, the paper showing through.
Large areas of the paper left completely unpainted to stand for mist,
water and sky. Muted and drained palette - ink black, warm grey, pale
ochre, washed-out green. One small area of vermilion red and no other
saturated colour. Flat frontal composition, no camera perspective, no
photographic realism. Quiet, plain, unspectacular.


=============================================================
THE NEGATIVE LIST - copy verbatim, every time
=============================================================

Negative: Japanese, ukiyo-e, vermilion lacquer bridge, torii, gold leaf,
stone lantern, pagoda, cherry blossom, koi, zen garden, raked gravel,
mountains, karst peaks, cliffs, airbrush, digital painting, concept art,
3D render, anime, oversaturated, neon, HDR, glow, bloom, lens flare,
photorealistic, painted to the edges, no white space, busy, ornate,
dragons, red paper lanterns everywhere, large mass of red foliage, red
canopy, text, characters, calligraphy, inscription, seal, signature,
watermark, frame, border.


=============================================================
WHAT JIANGNAN ACTUALLY LOOKS LIKE - read before generating
=============================================================

Every reference we looked at was a garden, a temple or a mountain
beauty spot. Ours is none of those. It is a WORKING TOWN.

  whitewashed walls going straight down into the canal, no bank
  black tile roofs, low, close together, sagging
  stone steps descending into the water at intervals
  flat wooden boats moored and empty
  low arched stone footbridges, plain, worn, unornamented
  willows, bare in the first lunar month
  laundry on poles over the water
  narrow lanes between houses, stone flagged, wet
  NO MOUNTAINS. Jiangnan is flat. The horizon is roofs and mist.

Get one photograph of Zhouzhuang, Wuzhen or Tongli in front of you
before you start, or the architecture will drift scenic.


=============================================================
STEP ONE - THE LIVING-WORLD KEY
=============================================================

Generate this FIRST and iterate until it is right. It sets the WARM end
of the game, which is the end we do not have a reference for yet - every
image we have is already the dead world.

  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A row of whitewashed water town houses at night with their walls going
  straight down into a canal, black tile roofs, warm lamplight in two of
  the windows, a flat wooden boat moored below. The lower third is calm
  water. Large areas of bare paper for mist. One small scrap of vermilion
  on a door.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

Judge it on: is at least a quarter of it bare paper, is the red a sliver
rather than a mass, is the palette drained apart from the lamplight,
does it look brushed rather than rendered, could you believe someone
lives there. Do not judge it on beauty yet.

Lock the seed once you have it, and keep this alongside the style key.


=============================================================
SCENE PLATES - generate each depth plane separately
=============================================================

Aspect 21:9 for ground planes. One element per plate, on bare paper,
so the engine can slide them for parallax.

  A long low row of whitewashed water town houses with black tile roofs
  seen from across a canal, receding gently into mist, small square
  windows, no people. The top half is bare unpainted paper. Isolated on
  bare paper with nothing above or below.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

CANAL BANK, MID PLANE
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A stone embankment along a canal with worn steps descending into the

  people. Isolated on bare paper.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

CANAL BANK, NEAR PLANE
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A few bare winter willow branches and a low stone kerb as dark
  silhouette shapes along the bottom edge, brushed in wet ink. Nothing

  [NEGATIVE LIST]

THE THREE BRIDGES - generate as a set, one session, same seed family

  One: [STYLE PREAMBLE] A modest low single arch stone footbridge, worn
  and plain and unornamented, seen from the side, its reflection in
  still water below. Isolated on bare paper. [NEGATIVE LIST]

  Two: [STYLE PREAMBLE] A taller humpbacked stone arch bridge with
  stepped sides and a plain stone post at each end, seen from the side,
  reflected in still water. Isolated on bare paper. [NEGATIVE LIST]

  Three: [STYLE PREAMBLE] A long covered bridge with a low tiled roof
  over its walkway, dark and enclosed, the far end lost in mist and not
  visible, seen from the side. Isolated on bare paper. [NEGATIVE LIST]

  Bridge three is where she sees her body. It must be the only one you
  cannot see through.

THE CITY GATE - the 摸釘 site, first five minutes
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A heavy closed wooden city gate with rows of large round bronze studs
  in a grid across it, framed by plain stone, seen straight on. No
  inscription, no plaque. Isolated on bare paper.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

HER DOOR - the last image in the game
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A plain wooden double door in a whitewashed wall at night, closed, a
  paper door god print freshly pasted on each leaf, crisp and new and
  uncreased. Warm lamplight falling from a small window beside it. One
  scrap of vermilion. Isolated on bare paper.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]


=============================================================
DOOR GODS - four wear states of the SAME pair
=============================================================

The lock system, DECISIONS 60. Generate the NEW pair first, then
produce the others as edits so it is recognisably one print decaying.

  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A pair of Chinese door god warrior figures printed on paper and pasted
  on a wooden door, one facing left and one facing right, armoured,
  stern, symmetrical, seen straight on. No text anywhere. Isolated on
  bare paper.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

  NEW     add: freshly pasted, crisp, bright, uncreased. This is the
          pair on her own door and the only perfect one in the game.
  INTACT  add: pasted some time ago, slightly dulled but whole.
  FADED   add: sun bleached and washed pale, colours weak, edges lifting.
  TORN    add: torn and hanging in strips, most of it missing.


=============================================================
CREATURES - eight to ten, per DECISIONS 86
=============================================================

Rule for all: they are folk images, stylised and almost heraldic, not
gory. Restraint reads as older and frightens more. In this medium that
means WET INK and a lot of bare paper around them.

水鬼 the drowned
  [STYLE PREAMBLE] A human figure standing waist deep in still water
  seen from the front, wet hair hanging flat over the face, arms at its
  sides, completely motionless, brushed in wet ink. Not gory. Bare paper
  above and around. [NEGATIVE LIST]

姑獲鳥 the night bird woman
  [STYLE PREAMBLE] A large bird with a woman's face and long hair, wings
  spread, seen frontally and symmetrically like an emblem, calm
  expression, not snarling. Brushed in ink. Isolated on bare paper.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

  This one is her. Keep it dignified. The ending is ruined if the player
  spent eight hours looking at a gargoyle.

畫皮 the painted skin
  [STYLE PREAMBLE] An empty human skin held up flat and spread like a
  garment on a line, a face painted on it, hollow and limp. Stylised and
  flat, not bloody. Isolated on bare paper. [NEGATIVE LIST]

石敢當 the stone
  [STYLE PREAMBLE] A short upright weathered stone slab set into the
  ground at the end of a lane, plain and blank with no inscription,
  leaning slightly. Isolated on bare paper. [NEGATIVE LIST]

五通神 the local god
  [STYLE PREAMBLE] A small seated enshrined folk deity figure, robed,
  frontal and symmetrical, with an expression that is pleasant but not
  kind. A crude village shrine idol. No text. Isolated on bare paper.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

=============================================================
QA - reject a plate if ANY of these are true
=============================================================

  Less than a quarter of it is bare unpainted paper.
  It is painted to all four edges.
  Red covers more than a small area.
  There is any saturated colour other than that red.
  There is a mountain in it.
  Anything about it reads Japanese.
  There is text, a character, a seal or a signature.
  It looks rendered, airbrushed or 3D rather than brushed.
  It is a composed scene rather than one isolated element.
  It looks like a beauty spot rather than a place people live.

The last two are the ones you will be tempted to let through.

Run tools/inkstate_test.py on any plate you are unsure about. If the
four panels look nearly identical, the plate is already the dead world
and has no colour left to lose.
