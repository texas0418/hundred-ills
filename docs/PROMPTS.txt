HUNDRED ILLS - IMAGE PROMPTS
Compiled 2026-08-19. Plain text so you can copy straight out.

Read the RULES section once. After that the prompts are copy-paste.


=============================================================
RULES - the four that actually matter
=============================================================

1. EVERY prompt is STYLE PREAMBLE + the plate description + NEGATIVE
   LIST. The preamble is copied verbatim every single time, never
   paraphrased, never trimmed to save space. Consistency across sixty
   plates comes from the preamble being byte-identical, not from the
   model remembering.

2. GENERATE PLATES, NOT PICTURES. Flat colour inside black outlines,
   nothing else. No lighting, no atmosphere, no weather, no gradient.
   The engine adds all of that. If a plate arrives looking moody and
   finished, it is a REJECT even though it is prettier, because the
   line and colour can no longer be pulled apart and the three-fires
   registration mechanic dies. This is the single most common way this
   pipeline will fail.

3. NO TEXT. No Chinese characters, no couplets, no signs, no seals, no
   signature. Models produce garbage characters and the one thing this
   game cannot survive is a nonsense couplet on a door. All type is set
   live in the app.

4. ONE ELEMENT PER PLATE, on empty background, for parallax. A scene is
   assembled in the engine from three to five separately generated
   plates. Do not ask for a finished composed scene.


=============================================================
THE STYLE PREAMBLE - copy verbatim, every time
=============================================================

Chinese New Year woodblock print in the Taohuawu style of late Qing
dynasty Suzhou. Hand-carved woodcut. Bold black keyline outlines of
even weight around every shape. Flat unmodulated areas of solid colour
filled inside the outlines. Provincial and slightly coarse, a cheap
village print, not refined court painting. Limited palette only: warm
buff paper, soot black line, one muted mineral green, one dull gamboge
yellow, and peach red used very sparingly. Flat frontal composition,
no camera perspective. Simple, legible, graphic, few elements.


=============================================================
THE NEGATIVE LIST - copy verbatim, every time
=============================================================

Negative: ink wash, sumi-e, watercolour, brush painting, gradient,
soft shading, ambient occlusion, cinematic lighting, glow, bloom, fog,
haze, depth of field, 3D render, digital painting, concept art, anime,
cel shading, photorealistic, oil painting, gold leaf, ornate, dragons,
red paper lanterns, cherry blossom, bamboo, pagoda, generic oriental
fantasy, text, characters, calligraphy, letters, watermark, signature,
frame, border, vignette.


=============================================================
STEP ONE - THE STYLE KEY
=============================================================

Generate this FIRST. Iterate until it is right, then keep it forever
and attach it as a style reference image to every later prompt. Nothing
else gets made until this one is correct.

  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A single stone arch bridge over a narrow canal, seen straight on.
  Whitewashed wall and dark tiled roof behind it. Nothing else in the
  frame. Empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

Judge it on: is the line an even carved weight, is every colour area
completely flat, are there exactly four or five colours, does it look
printed rather than painted. Do not judge it on beauty yet.

Lock the seed once you have it.

=============================================================
DOOR GODS - three wear states of the SAME pair
=============================================================

This is the lock system. Generate the intact pair first, then produce
the other two states from it as edits so it is recognisably the same
print decaying.

  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A pair of traditional door god warrior figures as a New Year print,
  one facing left and one facing right, armoured, holding weapons,
  stern symmetrical faces, standing frontally. Flat and graphic. No
  text anywhere. Isolated on empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

  State two, add: The print is sun faded and washed pale, its colours
  weak, edges lifting from the paper.

  State three, add: The print is torn in half and hanging in strips,
  most of it missing, only fragments still stuck to the wood.


=============================================================
THE AUSPICIOUS BABY - the most important image in the game
=============================================================

This has to be genuinely lovely. It is played straight for two hours
and it does all the work at the end. Do not make it creepy. Do not
add anything. The whole effect depends on it being a completely
ordinary, warm, mass-produced good-luck print.

  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A plump smiling baby boy in a peach red jacket sitting and holding a
  large carp in both arms, the classic auspicious New Year print
  subject. Cheerful, round, warm, benign. Flat and graphic. No text.
  Isolated on empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

Generate a lot of these. Pick the sweetest one.


=============================================================
CREATURES - five, per DECISIONS 29
=============================================================

Rule for all five: they are printed folk images, not monsters. A
village woodcut of a spirit is stylised and almost heraldic, not
gory. Restraint reads as older and frightens more.

水鬼 the drowned
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A human figure standing waist deep in still water, seen from the
  front, hair hanging flat and wet over the face, arms at its sides,
  completely motionless. Simple and stylised, not gory. Isolated on
  empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

姑獲鳥 the night bird woman
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A large bird with a woman's face and long hair, wings spread, seen
  frontally and symmetrically like a heraldic emblem. Calm expression,
  not snarling. Stylised folk print. Isolated on empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

  This one is her. Get it right and keep it dignified. The reveal at
  the end is ruined if the player has spent the game looking at a
  gargoyle.

畫皮 the painted skin
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  An empty human skin held up flat and spread out like a garment on a
  line, with a face painted on it, hollow and limp. Stylised and flat,
  not gory or bloody. Isolated on empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

石敢當 the stone
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A short upright weathered stone slab set into the ground at the end
  of a road, plain and blank with no inscription, slightly leaning.
  Isolated on empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

五通神 the local god
  [STYLE PREAMBLE]
  A seated enshrined folk deity figure, robed, frontal and symmetrical,
  with an expression that is pleasant but not kind. Small crude village
  shrine idol. No text. Isolated on empty buff background.
  [NEGATIVE LIST]

=============================================================
QA - reject a plate if ANY of these are true
=============================================================

  There is a gradient anywhere.
  There is a cast shadow, glow or bloom.
  Any colour area is modulated rather than flat.
  There are more than six colours.
  There is any text, character, seal or signature.
  The line weight varies dramatically or looks brush painted.
  It looks like a painting rather than a print.
  It has atmosphere baked in - fog, night, moonlight, warm lamp.
  It is composed as a finished scene rather than an isolated element.

The last two are the ones you will be tempted to let through.
