# Design

The authority is **`docs/DECISIONS.txt`**, not this file. This is the
one-page orientation; that is the contract.

## The spine

Three real folk systems are the architecture. Nothing here is invented.

| System | Folklore | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Space | 走三桥, cross three bridges | Three depth layers / three districts |
| Time | 更點, five watches × five points | 25 chapters of ~20 min |
| Life | 三把火, the three fires | Not health — denial |

## The fires

A living person carries three flames, one on each shoulder and one on the
crown; the dead cannot see you while they burn. They go out if someone taps
your shoulder, if you turn your head sharply, if you answer to your name in
the dark. They relight at a hearth, brazier, lamp, oven, or close to a
living body.

- **Three fires** — reads living. Door gods let her pass. Houses, hearths
  and people are open. The dead hide, so the spirit layer is shut.
- **Zero fires** — reads dead. Every warded door is shut. The dead stop
  hiding, and everywhere the wards lapsed is open. No reflection.

Each state opens what the other closes, so one town is two towns, rendered
from one set of plates by registration drift. The core verb is not *walk*,
it is *decide how alive to be*.

## The turn

She died in childbirth before the game begins. The child lived. 血湖地獄
consigns women who die in childbirth to a hell of blood, and 破血湖 — the
rite that releases them — is performed by their children. Hers was never
performed. The only person who can perform it is eleven days old.

So her love, her only hope of release, and 姑獲鳥's appetite for children
are one motion, not three motives.

## The end

Her own door. Door gods pasted eleven days ago, the only perfect pair left
in the game. **They hold.** Three fires was a costume, not a key. She gets
in only by being called past her own protection by her family, which is
what 叫魂 is.

Three endings: she answers, she waits until dawn, or — on the second walk —
she goes to the temple for the release her family never bought her.
